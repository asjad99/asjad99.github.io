# Succinct

Applied cryptography company (founded 2022; CEO Uma Roy, co-founder John Guibas). Makes SP1, a [[zkvm]], and runs
the Succinct Prover Network, a market for proof computing paid in the PROVE token. Raised $55M led by Paradigm (2024).

## Timeline
- 2024-08 SP1 live (src-0002).
- 2025-05 SP1 Hypercube: ~93% of Ethereum blocks proved in under 12 s (vendor claim).
- 2025-08 Prover Network + PROVE token mainnet.
- 2026-02 Optimism preferred partner (src-0003). 2026-03 Google Quantum AI uses SP1 (src-0005).
- 2026-04 ZCAM launched (src-0007). 2026-05 Base goes live with SP1 (src-0004).

## Open questions
- Past SP1 soundness bugs (fixed). PROVE price far below peak; usage and token price diverge.
