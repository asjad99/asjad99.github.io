# Orchid

Embryo [[embryo-screening|whole-genome screening]] company founded by Noor Siddiqui (Thiel Fellow, Stanford CS).
Reads ~99% of an embryo's genome from ~5 cells (company claim); screens 1,200+ single-gene conditions and gives
[[polygenic-risk-scores]].

## Facts
- Launched commercially Dec 2023; $2,500 per embryo on top of IVF (src-0010).
- ~100 US clinics by 2025 (src-0011). $12M raised; backers include Anne Wojcicki, Brian Armstrong, Vitalik Buterin,
  Balaji Srinivasan; George Church adviser.
- Motivation: founder's mother has retinitis pigmentosa.

## Criticism
- Amplification errors from few cells; disputed 2024 validation paper (src-0011).
- Polygenic scores weak for individuals and across ancestries.
