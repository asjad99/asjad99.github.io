# Embryo screening (preimplantation genetic testing)

During IVF, a few cells are taken from each embryo and tested before one is transferred. Standard tests (PGT-A)
count chromosomes and read well under 1% of the genome. Whole-genome screening reads almost all of it.

## Key points
- Two very different uses: finding single-gene diseases (strong evidence, narrow) and ranking by
  [[polygenic-risk-scores]] (weak evidence, easy to oversell).
- Technical risk: DNA from ~5 cells must be amplified, which can add errors (src-0011).
- Ethics debate: reproductive choice vs. eugenics concerns.
