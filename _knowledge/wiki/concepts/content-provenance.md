# Content provenance

Proving where a photo or file came from, instead of guessing whether it is fake. As AI makes fakes cheap, detection
gets weaker; signing content at capture gets more valuable.

## Key points
- [[succinct]]'s ZCAM (Apr 2026) signs a photo when it is taken (src-0007).
- Succinct's benchmark: top AI-image detectors fell from ~90–98% to ~11–36% after simple blur or compression
  (vendor benchmark).
- Limits: relies on phone secure hardware; SDK described as unaudited.
