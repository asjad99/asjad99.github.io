# Polygenic risk scores

A polygenic risk score adds up many small genetic effects to estimate risk of a common disease (e.g. diabetes,
some cancers, schizophrenia). Scores describe groups well but predict individuals poorly.

## Key points
- Different scoring methods often pick different "best" embryos; no pair agreed on the top embryo more than half the
  time in a simulation (Namba, ESHG 2024, not peer-reviewed; src-0012).
- Up to ~half as accurate for some non-European ancestries, because training data is mostly European (src-0011).
- ACMG says embryo use is unproven and should not be offered clinically (src-0011).
- Psychiatric Genomics Consortium objected to its data being used for embryo selection (src-0013).
