# Verification and trust

A theme across the wiki: new tools let people *check* things instead of *trusting* them.

- [[zero-knowledge-proofs]]: check a computation without redoing it or seeing secrets.
- [[content-provenance]]: check where a photo came from instead of guessing if it's fake.
- [[embryo-screening]]: read information that used to be invisible, though the reading can be noisy
  ([[polygenic-risk-scores]]).

Open question: when does more information help a decision, and when do precise-looking numbers mislead?
